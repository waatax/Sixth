# Unit 2: Past Tense Stories (過去式動詞與冒險故事)

To tell stories about yesterday, last weekend, or our childhood, we need the Past Tense (過去式)!

---

## 🕰️ Core Concept 1: Regular & Irregular Past Tense Verbs

### 1. Regular Verbs (規則動詞變化: +ed)
*   Most verbs: *play $\rightarrow$ play**ed**, watch $\rightarrow$ watch**ed***
*   Ending in 'e': *live $\rightarrow$ liv**ed**, like $\rightarrow$ lik**ed***
*   Consonant + 'y': *study $\rightarrow$ stud**ied**, cry $\rightarrow$ cr**ied***
*   Short vowel + consonant: *stop $\rightarrow$ stop**ped**, plan $\rightarrow$ plan**ned***

### 2. Common Irregular Verbs (高頻不規則動詞必背)

| Base Form (原形) | Past Form (過去式) | Meaning (中文意思) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **go** | **went** | 去 |
| **see** | **saw** | 看見 |
| **eat** | **ate** | 吃 |
| **have / has** | **had** | 有 / 吃 |
| **do / does** | **did** | 做 |
| **make** | **made** | 製作 |
| **buy** | **bought** | 買 |
| **take** | **took** | 拿 / 搭乘 / 花費 |

---

## ❓ Core Concept 2: Questions & Negatives in the Past

### 1. Past Tense with 'Did' (助動詞 did 的問句與否定句)
*   **Rule**: 只要出現了助動詞 did / didn't，後面的動詞必須**還原成原形動詞**！
*   **Negative**:
    *   *I **didn't see** the movie yesterday.* (⭕ 正確)
    *   *I didn't saw the movie yesterday.* (❌ 錯誤)
*   **Question**:
    *   *A: **Did** you **go** to the zoo last Sunday?*
    *   *B: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.*

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: *Did you bought the new book yesterday?*
*   ⭕ **Correct**: *Did you **buy** the new book yesterday?* (Did 出現，動詞變原形！)
