# Unit 1: Daily Routines & Time Management (日常生活與時間表達)

Learning English is about communicating your life! In this unit, we learn how to talk about our daily schedules, ask about time, and express frequency.

---

## ⏰ Core Concept 1: Telling Time & Asking Questions

### 1. How to ask and answer time
*   **Question**: *What time is it?* or *Do you have the time?*
*   **Answers**:
    *   It's seven o'clock. (7:00)
    *   It's seven fifteen. / It's a quarter past seven. (7:15)
    *   It's seven thirty. / It's half past seven. (7:30)
    *   It's seven forty-five. / It's a quarter to eight. (7:45)

### 2. Time Prepositions (時間介系詞)
*   **at** + specific time: *at 6:30 AM, at noon, at midnight*
*   **in** + morning/afternoon/month/year: *in the morning, in July*
*   **on** + days/dates: *on Monday, on October 10th*

---

## 📅 Core Concept 2: Daily Routine Activities & Frequency Adverbs

### 1. Common Daily Activities (常見日常活動)
*   *wake up / get up* (醒來 / 起床)
*   *brush my teeth / wash my face* (刷牙 / 洗臉)
*   *have breakfast / lunch / dinner* (吃三餐)
*   *go to school / do my homework* (去上學 / 寫功課)
*   *take a shower / go to bed* (洗澡 / 上床睡覺)

### 2. Frequency Adverbs (頻率副詞階梯)
$$\text{Always (100\%)} > \text{Usually (80\%)} > \text{Often (60\%)} > \text{Sometimes (40\%)} > \text{Seldom (20\%)} > \text{Never (0\%)}$$

*   **Sentence Pattern**:
    $$\text{Subject} + \text{Frequency Adverb} + \text{Verb}$$
    *   *I **always** walk to school.* (我總是走路去上學。)
    *   *She **sometimes** reads books in the library.* (她有時在圖書館看書。)

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: *I go to school at the morning.*
*   ⭕ **Correct**: *I go to school **in** the morning.* (早中晚時段要用 in the...！)
