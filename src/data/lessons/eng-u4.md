# Unit 4: Food, Health & Body Care (身體保健與看醫生英語)

Taking care of your health is vital. Let's learn body parts, healthy eating habits, and doctor visit dialogues!

---

## 🩺 Core Concept 1: Body Parts & Illness Expressions (身體部位與症狀)

### 1. Parts of the Body
*   *head* (頭), *eye* (眼睛), *ear* (耳朵), *nose* (鼻子), *throat* (喉嚨)
*   *shoulder* (肩膀), *stomach* (胃/肚子), *knee* (膝蓋), *foot/feet* (腳)

### 2. Expressing Symptoms (表達不舒服)
*   **Pattern**: *I have a / an + [symptom]*
    *   *I have a headache.* (我頭痛。)
    *   *I have a stomachache.* (我肚子痛。)
    *   *I have a sore throat.* (我喉嚨痛。)
    *   *I have a fever / a cold.* (我發燒 / 感冒了。)

---

## 🥗 Core Concept 2: Healthy Habits & Doctor Dialogue

### 1. At the Doctor's Office (看醫生對話)
*   **Doctor**: *What's the matter? / How are you feeling today?*
*   **Patient**: *I feel dizzy and I have a bad cough.*
*   **Doctor's Advice**:
    *   *Take this medicine three times a day after meals.* (飯後服藥，一天三次。)
    *   *Drink plenty of warm water.* (多喝溫開水。)
    *   *Get enough sleep and take a good rest.* (充足睡眠並好好休息。)

### 2. Nutrition & Food Groups (健康營養素)
*   *Vegetables and fruits* (蔬菜與水果) - rich in vitamins and fiber!
*   *Proteins* (蛋白質): eggs, milk, fish, tofu
*   *Grains* (全穀雜糧): brown rice, oats, whole wheat bread

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: *I have a tooth pain.*
*   ⭕ **Correct**: *I have a **toothache**.* (頭痛 headache, 胃痛 stomachache, 牙痛 toothache 都有一致的 -ache 字尾！)
