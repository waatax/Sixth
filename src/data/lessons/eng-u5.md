# Unit 5: Festivals, Holidays & World Cultures (多元節慶與世界文化)

Holidays bring people together across different cultures. Let's compare traditional Taiwanese and Western holidays in English!

---

## 🏮 Core Concept 1: Taiwanese Traditional Festivals (臺灣傳統節慶)

### 1. Lunar New Year (農曆新年 / 春節)
*   **Customs**: *clean the house* (大掃除), *have a reunion dinner* (吃年夜飯), *get red envelopes with lucky money* (拿壓歲錢), *set off firecrackers* (放鞭炮).
*   **Symbolic Food**: *dumplings* (元寶/水餃 for wealth), *fish* (年年有餘 for surplus), *rice cakes* (年糕 for promotion).

### 2. Dragon Boat Festival (端午節)
*   **Customs & Food**: *eat rice dumplings / zongzi* (吃肉粽), *watch dragon boat races* (看划龍舟), *wear scented sachets* (戴香包), *stand eggs at noon* (立蛋).

### 3. Moon Festival / Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋節)
*   **Customs & Food**: *eat mooncakes and pomelos* (吃月餅和柚子), *barbecue with family* (烤肉), *gaze at the full moon* (賞滿月).

## 🎄 Core Concept 2: Western Holidays & Traditions (西方重要節慶)

| Holiday | Date | Key Vocabulary & Traditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Halloween** | October 31 | *jack-o'-lantern* (南瓜燈), *costumes* (裝扮), *Trick or treat!* (不給糖就搗蛋) |
| **Thanksgiving** | 4th Thursday of Nov | *roast turkey* (烤火雞), *pumpkin pie* (南瓜派), giving thanks for blessings |
| **Christmas** | December 25 | *Santa Claus* (聖誕老人), *Christmas tree* (聖誕樹), exchanging gifts, singing carols |
| **Easter** | Spring | *Easter Bunny* (復活節兔), *Easter egg hunt* (尋找彩蛋) |

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: *I celebrate Christmas on December.*
*   ⭕ **Correct**: *I celebrate Christmas **in** December.* / *I celebrate Christmas **on** December 25th.* (只有月份用 in，有具體日期用 on 喔！)
