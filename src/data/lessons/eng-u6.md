# Unit 6: Reading Comprehension & Phonics Mastery (英語閱讀理解與字彙策略)

Reading opens the door to the world! In this unit, we master strategies to read English passages with confidence.

---

## 📖 Core Concept 1: Smart Reading Strategies (閱讀策略三部曲)

### 1. Skimming (略讀抓主旨)
*   Quickly look over the **title, headings, pictures, and the first & last sentence** of each paragraph.
*   Goal: Understand the **Main Idea (中心主旨)** before reading every single word.

### 2. Scanning (掃讀找細節)
*   Look for specific keywords, numbers, names, or dates (Who, When, Where, How much).
*   Example: If the question asks *"When was the bridge built?"*, scan directly for numbers and years!

### 3. Context Clues (利用前後文推敲生字)
*   Don't panic when you see a new word!
*   Look at the surrounding sentence: Is it a feeling? An action? An object?
*   *Example*: *"The dog was **famished** because it hadn't eaten for two days."* $\rightarrow$ *famished* means *very hungry*!

---

## 🔤 Core Concept 2: High-Frequency Phonics & Word Roots

### 1. Common Word Endings (常見字尾變化)
*   **-tion / -sion** (名詞結尾): *action, station, decision*
*   **-ful / -less** (形容詞結尾: 充滿 / 缺乏): *careful (細心) $\leftrightarrow$ careless (粗心)*
*   **-ly** (副詞結尾): *quick $\rightarrow$ quickly, slow $\rightarrow$ slowly*

### 2. Transition Words (文章連接詞)
*   *However / But* (轉折: 然而、但是)
*   *In addition / Furthermore* (遞進: 此外、而且)
*   *Therefore / As a result* (因果: 因此、結果)

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: Reading word-by-word with a dictionary every 5 seconds.
*   ⭕ **Correct**: Read the whole paragraph first to understand the big picture!
