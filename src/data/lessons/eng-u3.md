# Unit 3: Places & Asking for Directions (方位介系詞與問路指路)

Never get lost again! Learn how to navigate a map and give clear directions in English.

---

## 🗺️ Core Concept 1: Prepositions of Place (方位介系詞)

*   **next to** (在...旁邊): *The bookstore is next to the post office.*
*   **between A and B** (在 A 和 B 之間): *The bakery is between the bank and the park.*
*   **across from / opposite** (在...對面): *The hospital is across from the school.*
*   **on the corner of** (在...轉角處): *The coffee shop is on the corner of Main Street.*
*   **in front of / behind** (在...前面 / 後面): *There is a bus stop in front of the station.*

---

## 🚶 Core Concept 2: Asking & Giving Directions (問路與指路實用句型)

### 1. Polite Ways to Ask for Directions (禮貌問路)
*   *Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?* (請問如何前往火車站？)
*   *Excuse me, where is the nearest convenience store?* (請問最近的便利商店在哪裡？)
*   *Can you tell me the way to the museum?* (你能告訴我去博物館的路嗎？)

### 2. Giving Directions (清楚指路)
*   **Go straight** for two blocks. (直走兩個街區。)
*   **Turn left / Turn right** at the second traffic light. (在第二個紅綠燈左轉 / 右轉。)
*   It's **on your right / on your left**. (它就在你的右手邊 / 左手邊。)
*   You **can't miss it**! (你一定不會錯過的！)

---

## 🧠 Common Mistakes (迷思大破解)

*   ❌ **Incorrect**: *Turn to the left at the bank.*
*   ⭕ **Correct**: *Turn left at the bank.* (turn left / turn right 中間不需要加 to 喔！)
